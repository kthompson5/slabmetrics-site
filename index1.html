How It’s Different

We provide clarity, not encapsulation or authentication. Our Suggested Grades are independent opinions using a consistent rubric.

Surface

Scratches, print lines, gloss, stains, dimples, roller marks.

Centering

Front and back ratios; borders and design guides considered.

Edges

Average of all four edges; chipping, fray, factory cuts.

Corners

Average of all four corners; sharpness, roll, touches.
